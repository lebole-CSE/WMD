Study Tips

  • Set clear study goals before each session.
  • Create a study timetable and stick to it.
  • Study in a quiet, distraction-free environment.
  • Keep your phone away or on silent mode.
  • Study for 25–50 minutes, then take a 5–10 minute break.
  • Read your notes actively instead of passively.
  • Test yourself regularly using active recall.
  • Review topics frequently using spaced repetition.
  • Practice with past papers and sample questions.
  • Make short summary notes for each topic.
